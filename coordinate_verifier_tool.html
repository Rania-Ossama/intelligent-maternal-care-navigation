Coordinate Verification Methods

Why do coordinates sometimes look identical?
In raw datasets, administrative agencies (like MOHP) frequently place multiple hospitals at a single governorate directorate centroid. Our pipeline linked and snapped 50 of these to verified Google Maps rooftop pins.

Why are the numbers so close together?
Upper Egypt's population resides inside a narrow 2 to 15 km ribbon along the Nile River. In decimal degrees, 0.01 degree equals ~1.1 km. Adjacent facilities in the same city will naturally share the first 2-3 decimal digits.

Three Guaranteed Verification Methods:

  1. High-Resolution Satellite Inspection: Switch the map layer to ESRI Satellite (top-right layer icon). Zoom into level 18. Verified rooftop pins sit directly on top of the hospital building.
  2. External Google Maps Cross-Reference: Click any pin, then click Google Maps (Exact Pin) and Search Google Maps. If the pin matches Google's registered place listing, coordinates are confirmed.
  3. Automated Reverse Geocoding & Administrative Intersect: Mathematical verification confirming that Point(lon, lat) falls within the official cadastral polygon of the assigned governorate.
